# Dual rates & expo

From **`model4.yml`** Inputs (`expoData`). Switch **SC**.

## Positions

| SC | Name on radio | Ail | Ele | Rud |
|----|---------------|-----|-----|-----|
| SC0 (↑) | High | 100% · expo 15 | 100% · expo 15 | 100% · expo 15 |
| SC1 (−) | High | 100% · expo 15 | 100% · expo 15 | 100% · expo 15 |
| SC2 (↓) | Low | **65%** · expo **30** | **60%** · expo **30** | 100% · expo **30** |

Screen text: `SC - Rates High/High/Low`.

## When to use which

| Situation | SC |
|-----------|-----|
| Learning hover / calm cyclic | **Low** |
| Normal flying | High |
| **Gyro / 6G board calibrations** | **High** (need full throw to enter cal) |

Rates soften stick feel — they do **not** replace leveling the swash or 6G hover cal.

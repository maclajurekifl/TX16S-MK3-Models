# Dual rates & expo

Starter rates for **Align 450 XL** — all positions **100% weight / 30% expo** so you have headroom; soften Low/Mid after the first flights.

## Rates (**SC**)

### Aileron, Elevator & Rudder

| Rates | Switch | Weight | Expo |
|-------|--------|--------|------|
| Low | SC↓ | 100% | 30% |
| Medium | SC- | 100% | 30% |
| High | SC↑ | 100% | 30% |

## Where it lives

**Inputs** → Ail / Ele / Rud → three lines each, gated by **SC**.
